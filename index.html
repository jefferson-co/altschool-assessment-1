Why I Joined AltSchool

I joined AltSchool Africa to study Fullstack Engineering because I want to become a Design Engineer.

As a designer, I’ve spent years creating interfaces and experiences in Figma and bringing websites to life with tools like Framer. But I’ve always wanted to understand what happens beyond the design file.

I want to understand the code behind the interfaces I create, work more closely with engineers, and eventually be able to take an idea from concept to a working product myself.

For me, Design Engineering is about bringing design and technology together. I don’t want to choose between being a designer and being technical. I want to develop both skills and use them together to build better products.

AltSchool is helping me build the engineering foundation I need to take that next step.
